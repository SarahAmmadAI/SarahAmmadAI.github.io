Campus Lost & Found Management System

A campus lost-property system designed to organize lost reports, found items, ownership claims, and the return of belongings to their owners.

Project overview

Bringing lost-property records into one place.

The interface presents a central desk for the lost-property office, with navigation for lost items, found items, claims, and exam queries. Summary cards make the current workload easy to scan.

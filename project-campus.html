Overview

One place for campus operations.

The Swing desktop app provides an admin-oriented dashboard for managing students, courses, and facilities. A campus map view gives buildings a status such as active, busy, or closed, making the interface more visual than a list of records alone.

What I practised

Designing beyond a single class.

Concept / 01

Organising related responsibilities into classes and using encapsulation to keep the design manageable.

Concept / 02

Reusable behavior

Practising inheritance, polymorphism, and generics as tools for reducing duplication and expressing shared behavior.

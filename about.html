My journey

From fundamentals to intelligent systems.

I’m pursuing a BS in Artificial Intelligence at COMSATS University Islamabad. I’m working toward becoming an AI engineer, with a focus on building useful software and eventually applying AI to practical automation problems.

Java and object-oriented programming helped me develop a foundation in structuring code and thinking about system behavior. I put that into practice with my Smart Campus Management System, a desktop application for managing campus resources.

More recently, I’ve been learning backend engineering with Node.js and Express, including authentication, persistence, API documentation, and billing-related workflows. My FlyRank AI Fluency capstone, a Usage Metering & Billing Service, is one step in that journey.

I’m still learning — including C++ and core computer science concepts — and I value steady progress, clear fundamentals, and building projects that demonstrate what I understand.

A little about me

I like understanding systems.
Then making them useful.

I’m interested in the space where software engineering, practical problem-solving, and artificial intelligence meet. I learn by building, testing, documenting, and improving projects—not by treating a working demo as the end of the process.

My journey

Building a foundation, one project at a time.

I’m a BS Artificial Intelligence student at COMSATS University Islamabad. My work so far spans object-oriented programming, backend APIs, and AI-assisted workflows. My long-term direction is AI engineering, especially tools that make real tasks simpler and more efficient.

With Java and object-oriented programming, I practised turning requirements into a desktop application through my Smart Campus Management System. It helped me think about how classes, interfaces, and system behaviour fit together.

I’ve also explored backend engineering with Node.js and Express, including authentication, persistence, API documentation, usage tracking, quotas, and test-mode billing workflows. Alongside that, I built a Study Notes Summarizer Agent workflow using Claude Desktop and filesystem access through MCP.

I’m continuing to strengthen my C++ and computer science fundamentals while growing my practical experience. I value honest documentation, testing, and being able to explain why a solution works—as well as what still needs improvement.
